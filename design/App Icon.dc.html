Turn 1 · explorations
Three directions, drawn from the in-app palette
At the start of this exploration, assets/icon.png was the stock Expo placeholder; selected production direction 2a above now replaces it. All three use the game's own tokens — midnight #0C0F1A, gold #F2A33C, mint #57D9A3 — so the icon and the first screen feel like one product. Each is shown at 1024, 180 and 60 px; the 60 is the one that has to survive a crowded home screen.
1a Bowl & Bars
The steam is a revenue chart. Reads as a ramen bowl instantly at any size, and the mint bar is the same mint the MRR meter uses. Safest of the three, and the most obviously a game about noodles and money.
