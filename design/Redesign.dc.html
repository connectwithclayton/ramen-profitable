Turn 3 · redesign
Same tokens, fewer boxes, one hero per screen
Three structural moves, applied across every screen. One: corresponding emoji out, a drawn icon set in — same 24px grid, 2px stroke, gold when active; unmatched meanings stay emoji as enumerated in the icon-set contract below. Two: the card-in-card-in-card stack collapses; section headers sit directly on the midnight ground and only content that is genuinely a unit keeps a border. Three: each screen commits to a single hero — the quit-your-job rail, the compile ring, the cash balance, the feed — and everything else gets demoted. Palette and copy come straight from theme.ts and content.ts. A handful of numbers are invented for the mock and are not in the source yet: the lifetime price, the daily cash rate, the MRR trend arrow, the build string and the unread count.
