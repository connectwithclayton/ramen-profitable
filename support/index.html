Frequently asked questions

What is Ramen Profitable?

It is an idle game about building fictional apps. Write code, manage energy, survive simulated App Review, grow fictional monthly recurring revenue, and try to quit the day job at $2,000 MRR.

How do offline earnings work?

Your live fictional apps keep earning while the game is closed. Offline earnings are capped at eight hours. The optional Go Indie unlock doubles those offline earnings; it does not remove the eight-hour cap.

Is Go Indie a subscription?

No. Go Indie is an optional one-time in-app purchase that permanently doubles your character's offline earnings. The current offer and price are shown in the purchase screen before you confirm.

How do I restore Go Indie?

Open the Store screen and choose Restore Purchases. Make sure the device is online and signed in to the same store account used for the purchase. If it still does not restore, email support with the device platform and what the game shows.

Where is my game progress stored?

Game progress is stored locally on the device. Deleting the app or clearing its app data may remove that progress.
